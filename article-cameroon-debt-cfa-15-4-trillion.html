Cameroon's Debt Has Hit CFA 15.4 Trillion or 26 Billion Dollars

By Dr. David Makongo · May 29, 2026

RE: CAMEROON'S DEBT HAS HIT CFA 15.4 TRILLION OR 26 BILLION DOLLARS

CAMEROONIANS, THIS IS THE MOMENT TO UNDERSTAND ONE THING:

Division is no longer a political mistake. It is our collective prison.

Anglophones alone cannot save Cameroon.
Francophones alone cannot save Cameroon.
Politicians alone cannot save Cameroon.

Only a united people can save themselves.

The same system that humiliates the ordinary citizen also traps those inside it. Our brothers in prison are not free. Our brothers in uniform are not free. Our youths without jobs are not free. Even many of those pretending to control the system are themselves chained by fear, corruption, silence, and survival.

If the people are not free, nobody is truly free.

Cameroon does not need more tribal arrogance, regional hatred, political jealousy, or empty posturing. Cameroon urgently needs unity, courage, forgiveness, and a common purpose.

Freedom for political prisoners is urgent.
Justice is urgent.
Dignity is urgent.
Hope for the youth is urgent.

The future generation must stop inheriting fear as culture and division as identity. Our youths deserve a nation where hard work matters more than connections, where truth is not punished, and where citizens are treated like human beings — not livestock surviving under permanent suffering.

This generation must become the generation that refused to hate each other.
The generation that united peacefully.
The generation that reclaimed its rights together.
The generation that rebuilt Cameroon together.

No violence.
No revenge.
No hatred.

Only courage.
Only unity.
Only a fearless people determined to stand together and peacefully demand a better nation for all.

A divided people remain weak.
A united people become impossible to enslave.

If the people decide to do nothing, this debt will not be paid by those who ate the money, it will be passed on to unborn generations who absolutely inherit nothing gained out of this monster debt.

Thank you.

Dr. David Makongo
