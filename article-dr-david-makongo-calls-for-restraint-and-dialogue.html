Dr. David Makongo Calls for Restraint and Dialogue in the Face of Social Tensions

By Dr. David Makongo · Apr 24, 2026

Dr. David Makongo Calls for Restraint and Dialogue in the Face of Social Tensions

In an opinion piece published in a context of growing tensions, Dr. David Makongo calls for a surge of collective responsibility, advocating for restraint, dialogue, and mutual respect as the foundations of a peaceful society.

In this text with strong civic significance, the author warns against the excesses of public debate, particularly on social media, where hostile remarks and personal attacks are becoming increasingly common. According to him, such behavior deepens social divisions and weakens national cohesion.

Dr. Makongo emphasizes the need for citizens to demonstrate responsibility in both their words and actions. He notes that freedom of expression, although essential, cannot justify insults or hate speech. In his view, only a culture of dialogue can help overcome differences and preserve national unity.

Addressing the issue of relations with authority, the author recalls that respect for state institutions is a fundamental pillar of order and stability. However, he qualifies this point by stressing that such respect must be mutual: leaders, he says, have a duty to act with integrity, humility, and a strong sense of responsibility in order to earn the trust of citizens.

In the face of escalating tensions, Dr. Makongo calls for urgent de-escalation and a return to reason. He encourages the establishment of a climate of mutual respect between those in power and the public, an essential condition for building a more just and balanced society.

In conclusion, the author states that “peace, unity, and respect must guide us on the path forward,” reminding readers that a nation that fosters dignity in its exchanges emerges stronger, both internally and internationally.

Through this opinion piece, Dr. David Makongo delivers a message that is both clear-eyed and unifying, inviting everyone to contribute, at their own level, to preserving social harmony.
