International Women’s Day: Dr. David Makongo’s tribute message to women worldwide

By Dr. David Makongo · Mar 9, 2026

On the occasion of International Women’s Day celebrated on March 8, Dr. David Makongo delivered a strong message of tribute and recognition to women across the world, praising their courage, resilience, and decisive role in society.

In his statement, Dr. Makongo highlights the sacrifice and daily commitment of many women who—often behind the scenes—work for the well-being of their families and communities. In his view, these women embody “the heart of the home and the backbone of the world,” a reminder of their essential contribution to the stability and development of societies.

He also emphasized women’s determination in the face of life’s challenges. For Dr. Makongo, those who keep moving forward despite obstacles demonstrate remarkable strength. He describes them as builders of their own destiny, able to turn trials into opportunities and challenges into successes.

“To the women who remain standing when the wind blows against them—who adjust their sails and continue on their path—your resilience is a true force of nature,” he said, paying tribute to the adaptability and perseverance that characterize so many women around the world.

To conclude, Dr. David Makongo shared a special thought for his wife, Mrs. Silo Makongo, while joining men around the world in wishing all women a meaningful celebration of International Women’s Day.

This message is both a tribute and a call to better recognize the value and contribution of women in every area of social, economic, and family life.
