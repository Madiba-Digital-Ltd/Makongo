Pan-African Distinction: Dr. David Makongo Crowned Philanthropist of the Decade

Yaounde - A major recognition has crowned the journey of Dr. David Makongo, President-Founder of Makongo Family Charity (MAFAC). The humanitarian figure has been officially named "Laureate of the Pan-African Humanitarian Award, Philanthropist of the Decade," as part of the Decennial Awards marking the 10th anniversary of the media outlet The Voice.

This prestigious distinction honors a decade of sustained commitment and concrete actions in support of vulnerable populations across the African continent. Through MAFAC, Dr. Makongo has distinguished himself with high-impact interventions, particularly in prisons, orphanages, and underprivileged rural areas.

His initiatives have significantly contributed to improving living conditions in several key sectors, including health, education, and women’s empowerment. His work in support of children with special needs, in particular, is widely praised as a model of innovation and effectiveness in the humanitarian sector.

According to the organizing committee, this distinction rewards not only the scale of the results achieved but also the innovative approach adopted by the laureate in implementing his programs at an international level. It also reflects recognition of a consistent commitment guided by values of altruism and solidarity.

The selection process for the Decennial Trendsetters awardees is based on a rigorous methodology, combining public voting across the ten regions of Cameroon and evaluation by an independent jury made up of civil society figures and experienced journalists. This approach gives the award both popular and professional legitimacy.

The official award ceremony is scheduled for May 30, 2026, in Yaounde, at the UNAFF Banquet Hall, as part of the celebrations marking the 10th anniversary of The Voice. The event will be held under the high patronage of the President of the National Communication Council and will bring together several institutional, media, and diplomatic stakeholders.

For the organizers, this celebration aims to highlight individuals whose commitment contributes tangibly to the development of the continent. Despite budget constraints mentioned by the committee, the ambition remains to deliver a ceremony worthy of the distinctions awarded, with national and international media coverage.

With this recognition, Dr. David Makongo strengthens his status as a leading figure in humanitarian action in Africa. This honor not only celebrates his achievements but also inspires a new generation of individuals committed to serving communities.
