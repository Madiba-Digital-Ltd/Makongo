Who is Dr. David Makongo

Dr. David Makongo: Trailblazing African Leader in Law, Business and Philanthropy.

Dr. David Makongo stands as an exemplar of leadership, commitment and innovation, forging an indelible legacy within Africa's legal, business and philanthropic spheres. As an internationally acclaimed business mining lawyer, expert mediator and devoted philanthropist, his passion is deeply rooted in enhancing the human experience. Guided by an unyielding mission to address political, economic and social challenges through nonviolent, peaceful, humanitarian and empowerment-driven means, Dr. Makongo's impact resonates far beyond borders.

His journey is marked by numerous achievements, including the highest academic accolades, notably the distinguished Highest Academic Award in Negotiations of Natural Resources Agreements from Denver University Sturm College of Law. Possessing an Honorary Doctorate of Philosophy in Humanities and holding a Master of Laws specializing in Natural Resources Environmental Law & Policy, Mining, Oil and Gas and Negotiation of Natural Resources Agreements, his academic prowess is paralleled only by his dedication.

With a profound professional trajectory, Dr. Makongo's trailblazing career spans key roles such as Legal Advisor for Cameroon National Petroleum Depot (SCDP), Director for African Affairs at Electrum USA LTD. and Founding Partner of Afro-American Consortume (AAC-3). His prowess extends to the helm of Makongo & African Partners Limited, where for over a decade, he has channeled his expertise in mining and oil & gas issues, fostering responsible practices, conducting feasibility studies and advocating for environmental and social impact assessments.

Furthermore, as Chairman of the Board at Fako Mining & Management (FAKOMM) LLC, he underscores his commitment to multinational service consultancy, uplifting the Republic of Guinea. Dr. David Makongo's multi-faceted contributions encompass legal advisory, contract negotiation, anti-corruption training, risk analysis and community liaison. In the dynamic realm of energy, infrastructure, metals and oil and gas, his strategic guidance has been pivotal.

As a visionary in law, a catalyst for sustainable business growth and a driving force for positive change, Dr. Makongo's journey is a testament to the transformative potential of dedication, expertise and compassion. Through his unceasing efforts, he illuminates a path for generations to come, inspiring progress, unity and enduring impact across the African continent and beyond.

Book

David Defeats Goliath by David Makongo

DAVID DEFEATS GOLIATH is a proverbial walk down the mountains and valleys of our lives. It is filled with wise counsel from across generations and different sages. It is predicated on the fact that one man with the right mind frame can win epic battles. Using his own life, while growing up in rural Southern Cameroons, Dr. David Makongo captures the moods of the young folks fighting oppression back home, and with snippets, boosts the waning morale of his Ambazonian brethren, who in their vast majority are asking questions whether the path to freedom they took is tenable.
